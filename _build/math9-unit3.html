Unit 3

Decimals & Averages

Prices per kilogram, splitting a bill, cutting lumber, figuring out your average hours at work: decimals are everywhere on the job. In this unit you'll multiply and divide decimals, round sensibly, and find the mean by hand and in a spreadsheet.

2Lessons

1Multiplying decimalsPrice × amount›
I can multiply decimals in everyday situations. Number 5
Multiply like whole numbers, then count the decimal places in both numbers. Your answer has that many decimal places. On a calculator, just check that the answer is reasonable.

Example 1

2.4 × 1.5: 24 × 15 = 360. There are 2 decimal places in total, so 3.60 = 3.6. Check: about 2 × 2 = 4 ✓

Example 2 At the deli

Ham costs $2.39 per 100 g. You buy 350 g, which is 3.5 × 100 g. 2.39 × 3.5 = 8.365 → $8.37.
2Dividing decimalsSplitting, sharing and cutting›
I can divide decimals, including by 2-digit numbers. Number 5
Dividing tells you how many fit or how much each gets. If you're dividing by a decimal, multiply both numbers by 10 (or 100) until you're dividing by a whole number.

Example 1 Cutting a board

How many 0.4 m shelves come from a 2.8 m board? 2.8 ÷ 0.4 → 28 ÷ 4 = 7 shelves.

Example 2 Splitting a bill

14 players split a $175.70 team dinner. 175.70 ÷ 14 = 12.55 → $12.55 each. Check: 14 × 12.55 = 175.70 ✓
3Is it reasonable?Estimate, then check›
I can check an answer by estimating. Number 2, 9
Before you trust a calculator, round to friendly numbers and estimate. The most common mistake is the decimal point in the wrong place. An estimate catches it.

Example

A worker says 18 boxes at $4.95 is $891. Estimate: 20 × $5 = $100. $891 is way too big. The real answer is 18 × 4.95 = $89.10. The decimal was in the wrong place.
4The mean (average)Add them up, divide by how many›
I can find the mean (average) of a set of numbers. Number 11
The mean is one number that represents a whole group. mean = total ÷ how many.

Example Average tips

A server's tips over 5 shifts: $42, $58, $36, $71, $48. Total = $255. 255 ÷ 5 = $51 a shift.

Estimate first

All the tips are between $36 and $71, so the mean must be somewhere in between. $51 makes sense.
5SpreadsheetsLet the computer add and average›
I can use a spreadsheet to find totals and averages. Statistics 8
In Google Sheets or Excel, every formula starts with =. =SUM(B2:B6) adds cells B2 to B6. =AVERAGE(B2:B6) finds their mean.

Decimals & Averages Cheat Sheet · Grade 9 Math

Multiplying: multiply like whole numbers, then count the total decimal places. 2.4 × 1.5 → 24 × 15 = 360 → 3.60

Dividing by a decimal: multiply both numbers by 10 until the divisor is whole. 2.8 ÷ 0.4 → 28 ÷ 4 = 7

Money: round to the hundredth (the cent). $8.365 → $8.37

Estimate first: round to friendly numbers. 18 × $4.95 ≈ 20 × $5 = $100. If your answer is far off, check the decimal.

Mean (average) = total ÷ how many. $42 + $58 + $36 + $71 + $48 = $255, and 255 ÷ 5 = $51.
