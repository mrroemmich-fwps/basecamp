Japan: Isolation & Adaptation Cheat Sheet · Grade 8 Social Studies

Worldview = beliefs + values + knowledge. Shaped by geography, time, family, culture, religion and contact with others.

Geography: 4 main islands, mostly mountains, surrounded by sea → natural protection, little farmland, a unique culture.

WhenWhat happened
1600s–1868Edo period: the shogun rules
1630sJapan closes its doors; small Dutch and Chinese trade only at Nagasaki
1853Commodore Perry's "black ships" arrive and demand trade
1868Meiji era begins: the emperor is restored and rapid change starts

Social structure: Emperor → Shogun → Daimyo → Samurai → Peasants, artisans, merchants

Meiji changes: railways, factories, schools, modern army, samurai class ended. Kept: language, Shinto and Buddhism, arts, respect for family.

Here at home: First Nations, Métis and Inuit worldviews were changed by contact, often by force (residential schools, banned ceremonies, land). Today languages and traditions are being revitalized.
