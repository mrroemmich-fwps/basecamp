Consumer Math Cheat Sheet · Grade 9 Math

Fraction → decimal: top ÷ bottom (3/8 = 0.375). Decimal → percent: × 100 (0.375 = 37.5%). Percent → fraction: over 100, then simplify (40% = 40/100 = 2/5).

Percent of an amount: change the percent to a decimal and multiply. 15% of $64 = 0.15 × 64 = $9.60. Round money to the nearest cent.
