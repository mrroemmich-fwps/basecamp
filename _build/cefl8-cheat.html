Job Offers & Pay Cheat Sheet · CEFL 8

Read every offer for: pay · hours (part-time or full-time) · start date · duties · benefits and extras

Pay formulas: weekly pay = hourly wage × hours per week · yearly pay = weekly pay × 52. This is gross pay, before deductions.

Total compensation = pay + bonuses + allowances + value of benefits (dental, glasses, prescriptions, paid vacation)
