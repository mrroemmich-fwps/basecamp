Unit 6

My Career Portfolio

A portfolio shows who you are and what you can do. In this unit you'll research a career using real Alberta tools, find out what reading and writing it takes, practise the habits employers look for, and put together a portfolio you can keep building through high school.

2Lessons

1Planning research5W + H questions›
I can plan and carry out research on a career. 3.1.1, 3.2.1
Good research starts with good questions. Use 5W + H: Who does this job? What do they do? Where do they work? When (hours, shifts)? Why is it needed? How do you get into it?

Example Career: electrician

What do electricians do? Where do they work? How long is the apprenticeship? What reading and math do they use on the job? How much do they earn in Alberta?
2Using ALISAlberta's career tool›
I can use ALIS to find the language skills a career needs. 1.1.4
ALIS (alis.alberta.ca) has an OCCinfo profile for hundreds of careers: duties, working conditions, education, wages and outlook. Many profiles also list the reading, writing and communication skills the job needs.

What to look for in a profile

  • Duties: what a typical day looks like
  • Working conditions: indoors, shifts, physical work
  • Educational requirements
  • Wage and salary in Alberta
  • Employment outlook: are workers needed?
3Notes and sourcesPoint form, in your own words›
I can take point-form notes and cite my sources. 3.3.2
Point-form notes use key words, not full sentences, and are in your own words. Always record where you found it (website name, title, date) so others can check it, and so you're not copying someone else's work.

Example

Source text: "Electricians typically complete a four-year apprenticeship program that combines on-the-job training with technical training."
Notes: • 4-yr apprenticeship • work + classes (ALIS, Electrician)
4Successful worker habitsWhat employers notice›
I can describe and show the behaviours of a successful worker. 5.2.1
Employers say these habits matter as much as skills: arrive on time, follow directions, ask for help when you need it, stay organized, prioritize tasks and take constructive feedback well.
🏔️ Avalanche HQ connection: These are the same habits that earn AvCash in class. You're already practising for the job site.
5Building the portfolioShow what you can do›
I can build a portfolio and a language learning plan. 1.1.4, 3.4.1
A career portfolio is a folder (paper or digital) that shows your best work, skills and goals. Add to it every year.

What goes in

  • A cover page with your name and a career goal
  • Your career research
  • 2–3 pieces of your best writing
  • Certificates, awards, volunteer hours
  • A language learning plan: one reading, writing or speaking skill you need for your goal, and how you'll build it
