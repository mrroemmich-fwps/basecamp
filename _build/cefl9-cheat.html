Getting Hired Cheat Sheet · CEFL 9

Decode a posting: highlight qualifications (must-haves) and skills · "an asset" = nice to have · match each one to your own experience.

Brand statement: I am a [quality] person who [skill or strength]. I want to [goal]. Use it on your résumé, in your cover letter and for "Tell me about yourself."

Tailor your résumé: put the most relevant experience first and use the posting's words. Your résumé shows your human capital.

Digital identity check: search your name · set accounts to private · "Would I be OK with my future boss seeing this?"

Self-advocacy: say your value, your needs and your availability clearly and honestly. Have stories ready: teamwork, solving a problem, a mistake you learned from.
