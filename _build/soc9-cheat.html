Resources, Trade & the Economy Cheat Sheet · Grade 9 Social Studies

WordMeaning
EconomyHow a society makes, shares and uses goods and services
ScarcityNot enough for everyone, so we make choices
Supply / demandHow much is available / how much people want it
Market economyBuyers and businesses make most decisions
Mixed economyBusinesses AND government make decisions (Canada)
Export / importSold to another country / bought from another country
TariffA tax on imported goods
Quality of lifeIncome, health, safety, environment, free time
Labour unionWorkers bargaining together (collective bargaining)

Supply and demand: high demand + low supply → price up · low demand + high supply → price down

Canada and the U.S.: the U.S. is Canada's biggest trading partner. Alberta sells oil, gas, beef and grain; buys cars, machinery and produce. CUSMA = the Canada–U.S.–Mexico trade agreement.

Resource decisions balance jobs and tax money against protecting land, water and communities.
